Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Richardson Partners, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates richardsonpartnersllc.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, submit our contact form, or participate in our SMS messaging program, Richardson Partners LLC SMS Notifications.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our website contact form, including your first name, last name, email address, mobile phone number (if provided), message content, and any SMS consent selections you choose. We may also collect standard technical data such as browser type, device information, and pages visited.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested real estate services
  • Communicate with you about property opportunities and transactions
  • Send SMS messages only if you have explicitly opted in to our messaging program
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

Mobile Information and Messaging Consent

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted solely for operational support and is not permitted for marketing or promotional use.

We may disclose information only when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

SMS / Text Messaging Terms & Opt-In Information

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from Richardson Partners, LLC through our website contact form at richardsonpartnersllc.com/contact, we collect your name, mobile phone number, and the consent preferences you select.

Program: Richardson Partners LLC SMS Notifications.

Purpose: We use your mobile information solely to send transaction-related updates, appointment confirmations, real estate service inquiries, property updates, and two-way customer support communications related to our services. We do not use your mobile information or messaging consent for unrelated marketing without your separate opt-in.

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your interactions with Richardson Partners, LLC.

Message and Data Rates: Message and data rates may apply for messages sent to you from us and to us from you.

Opt-Out & Help: You can cancel the SMS service at any time by texting STOP. After you send STOP, we will send you an SMS to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at thom@richardsonpartnersllc.com or (629) 206-6237.

Please see our Terms of Service for additional details about our SMS program.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We and our third-party service providers use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your interactions with our website. This includes browser type, operating system, IP address, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and links clicked. We use this information to maintain security, optimize website performance, analyze site traffic, and enhance your user experience. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent; however, some parts of our website may not function properly without cookies.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at thom@richardsonpartnersllc.com or (629) 206-6237.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
